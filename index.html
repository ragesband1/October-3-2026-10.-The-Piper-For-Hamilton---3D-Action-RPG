10. The Piper For Hamilton

Written by: Peter Pintago

Once upon a time in a highest village there lived a people of Hamilton. They had trashes outside their place. One day thousands of rats ate the left-over foods without premises.

When the people knew the particular evidence, they shouted: "Oh my, rats is a contagious disease affecting our health! Our gracious Piper, would you come to save us then let us go over the rats!"

Then the gracious Piper was present. He possessed a "Flute of Hypnosis" so that rats would follow whatever the Piper said to leave out the town.

When the rats had abandoned the town for left-over foods, people did not content with that experience — they did not congratulate nor reward the Piper. The Piper grew angry. He used his flute to hypnotize the children.

Hypnotized by the Piper's flute, the children followed him into the volcano so they would never see their families again. When the people learned their lesson, they wept in despair over their disobedience and broken promises to the gracious Piper.

Moral Lesson

"If you have a savior who saves you, please give him a reward. Don't underestimate treason or broken promises. In disobedience and mistrust, people suffer — even when there are no rats left."
